Privacy Policy

Deutsch Lernen: Der Die Das (also published as "German Go: Der Die Das")
Effective date: November 13, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the iOS app "Deutsch Lernen: Der Die Das" (the "App"), created by Ihsan Dincer (the "Developer") and provided free of charge with advertising. It explains what information is processed when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and the Developer does not collect, receive or store any personal information about you. We do not operate any servers that receive data from the App.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only (using Apple's UserDefaults):

  • Your selected language and quiz length
  • Your current quiz progress, so you can resume it later
  • Statistics per word (how often it was asked, answered correctly or incorrectly)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the Developer. It is deleted when you delete the App.

3. Pronunciation

Word pronunciation uses Apple's built-in text-to-speech engine, which runs on your device. No text is sent to the Developer.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Device information (e.g. device model, operating system version, app version)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data (e.g. ad views and clicks)
  • Diagnostic and performance data
  • Identifiers for vendors or advertising, where permitted

The App does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). Without this permission, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the App or to Google.

Google processes this data according to its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

5. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalized ads, which rely on limited data such as contextual information.

You can reset your choice at any time by deleting and reinstalling the App; you will then be asked again. You may also contact us at the email address below.

The legal basis for advertising-related processing is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) or, for non-personalized ads, Google's and the Developer's legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Since the Developer does not hold any personal data about you, requests concerning advertising data are best directed to Google; we are happy to help if you contact us.

7. Children

The App is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.
